# Steam Discussion Draft — KNOX NIGHTMARE

## Title

**KNOX NIGHTMARE: BLIND — What if Project Zomboid stopped feeling safe indoors? [B42.20 Horror Build]**

## Post

I've been building a Build 42.20 horror setup called **KNOX NIGHTMARE**, and the goal is simple:

**I want Project Zomboid to make you nervous again.**

Not by turning every zombie into a 100% sprinter.
Not by dumping 500 zombies on your spawn.
Not by making the game unfair for the sake of difficulty.

I want the fear to come from never being completely sure that you're safe.

The recommended first experience is called **BLIND**. It tells you the rules you need to install safely, but deliberately does not catalogue every threat or trigger.

What I will reveal:

- long pitch-black nights
- rare mixed-speed infected instead of predictable all-sprinters
- oppressive visuals, fog and atmosphere
- interiors that cannot be treated as permanently safe
- dangerous storms
- sound and physical-scene pressure
- a world that changes after you think you understand it
- scarce guns, ammo, medicine, fuel and reliable vehicles
- no minimap and no world map in BLIND
- save-safe native installers for **Windows and Linux/Steam Deck**
- normal Steam Workshop support with missing-mod detection

The part I'm most interested in is **psychological pressure**.

You hear something outside.
You don't know whether it matters.
The weather gets worse.
It's getting dark.
You have one flashlight battery left.
Then something hits the window.

And you're not sure if the thing you heard ten seconds ago is the same thing trying to get inside.

Some threats are intentionally undocumented. If I tell you exactly what can happen, when it can happen, and what every sound means, I ruin the point.

Experimental systems are tested separately instead of throwing incompatible horror mods into one pile. BLIND is stable-first: it does not secretly enable an unverified candidate just to make a better bullet list.

There are four profiles:

**BLIND** — recommended first playthrough, maximum uncertainty, stable SOLO stack.
**SOLO** — maximum horror and immersion.
**CO-OP** — scary, but built around hosted-MP compatibility.
**SERVER** — dedicated-server stability first, horror second.

Current platform support:

**Windows 10/11** — native PowerShell SOLO + hosted CO-OP installer.
**Linux** — native SOLO + CO-OP + dedicated-server tooling.
**Steam Deck / SteamOS** — uses the Linux local path.
**macOS** — manual/experimental for now; not claiming full support until the stack is tested there.

The project is public on GitHub as:

**DJTheNetrunna/the-blackout**

I'm looking for people who actually play horror-heavy Zomboid to tell me what would scare you more:

- a threat you only understand after surviving it?
- returning to a place you thought was cleared?
- noises that force a decision without giving an answer?
- oppressive fog and weather?
- zombies that behave differently in darkness?
- events that force you out of a comfortable base?

If you know a **Build 42.20 mod that genuinely made you uncomfortable**, drop it below. I'm willing to test weird stuff as long as it adds real horror instead of just cheap difficulty.

**The objective isn't to make Zomboid impossible. The objective is to make opening a door feel like a decision again.**
