# Compatibility Rules

1. Build 42.20.x is the supported baseline.
2. Build 41 save/config assumptions are not accepted without explicit revalidation.
3. Only `approved` manifest entries may enter `core` or `recommended`.
4. `candidate` entries are test-world only.
5. `hold` and `rejected` entries are never generated.
6. Third-party Workshop payloads stay on Steam.
7. Any mod that requires replacing dedicated-server root files is presumed non-reproducible until proven otherwise.
8. Back up before every Build/mod transition.
